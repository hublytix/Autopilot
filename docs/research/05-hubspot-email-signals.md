## 05. HubSpot email engagements: confirmed sends, reply detection, inbox logging, BCC, opt-out

The official sources for this section are HubSpot's public OpenAPI spec collection (commit 476fa1a, 2026-10-01) and HubSpot's Node SDK (`hubspot-api-nodejs`, tag 14.0.1). The sandbox blocked developers.hubspot.com, knowledge.hubspot.com and community.hubspot.com, so developer-doc wording is cited through a verbatim third-party scrape of developers.hubspot.com (github.com/teostereciu/mcpsynth, scraped 2026-04-13), and knowledge-base (KB) wording only through search summaries, which are not guaranteed to be verbatim. Together they show that every send, reply and opt-out signal Autopilot needs can be read on the Free tier with read-only scopes and without reading email content, but the brief needs one scope added and a different reply signal. Everything about the owner's HubSpot setup (BCC logging on Free/Starter, connected-inbox logging rules and their default) rests only on KB search summaries, so it is marked "Not officially documented" below and must be confirmed on Free and Starter test portals during WIRE_UP. The §5.7 live test is the runtime check.

**Confirmed as the brief assumes:**
- Logged one-to-one emails can be detected read-only, using metadata properties only (no subject or body), which fits Laws 2 and 4.
- The CRM Emails, Associations and Contacts read APIs are available on HubSpot's Free tiers.

**The sources correct the brief in five places:**
1. **§5.1 scopes:** reading email engagements needs `sales-email-read` in addition to `crm.objects.contacts.read`. It is a read scope, but the consent screen will show email access.
2. **§5.6 reply signal:** a contact "last replied" timestamp is not reliable. A reply is confirmed only by an `INCOMING_EMAIL` or `FORWARDED_EMAIL` engagement from the lead's address, associated to the contact after the first notification. `hs_sales_email_last_replied` is a positive-only fallback, and a missing engagement never proves "no reply".
3. **§5.5 BCC logging:** BCC logs only the owner's send (`EMAIL`), never the lead's reply. BCC-only owners get no automatic reply detection unless they forward replies to the forwarding address. The owner must also send FROM an address HubSpot knows.
4. **§5.6 opt-out property:** stop on `hs_email_optout` = `"true"` (scope `crm.objects.contacts.read`). Do not request `communication_preferences.read_write` (write-capable) or `communication_preferences.statuses.batch.read` (needs Marketing Hub Enterprise).
5. **§5.7 "the owner's email is logged":** that is not enough. Only a connected inbox with the "Log all emails to/from known contacts" rule logs both the compose-link send and the lead's reply; "Log replies only" does not cover compose-link sends.

**The sources extend the brief with:**
- exact endpoints, search body, operators and limits for email engagements, plus a 30-day logged-email count using `total`;
- a 2-call per-contact read (contact GET with `associations=emails`, then emails `batch/read`) that avoids search-index lag and also detects deletion and merges;
- a precise "confirmed send" rule (`EMAIL` direction, to the lead's address, after the notification);
- extra hard stops: hard-bounce and invalid-address properties, deleted contacts (HTTP 404) and merged contacts (new ID);
- a concrete two-leg design for the §5.7 live test and the §5.8 baseline.

### HS-EMAIL-SCOPES — Reading email engagements needs `sales-email-read` plus `crm.objects.contacts.read`
- **Brief:** §5.1 lists the minimum scopes as `oauth`, `crm.objects.contacts.read`, `forms`. It assumes reply, send and inbox-logging detection is possible with these; no email scope is named.
- **Resolves:** [VERIFY] §5.1 minimum scopes (the email-engagement part, needed by §5.6–5.9).
- **Verdict:** Corrected — high confidence.
- **Finding:**
  - Reading email engagement objects needs the scope `sales-email-read` **in addition to** `crm.objects.contacts.read`.
  - The current date-versioned Emails spec (rollout 424, `2026-09`) declares this on every read operation (`GET /crm/objects/2026-09/{objectType}`, `GET .../{objectId}`, `POST .../search`, `POST .../batch/read`): `security: [{"oauth2": ["crm.objects.contacts.read", "sales-email-read"]}, {"private_apps": ["crm.objects.contacts.read", "sales-email-read"]}]`. Both scopes sit in ONE security-requirement object, which in OpenAPI means both are required (AND).
  - The v3 spec (`/crm/v3/objects/emails...`) has empty per-operation security, so the 2026-09 spec is the authoritative scope statement. The 2026-09 Emails spec's oauth2 scope catalogue lists `sales-email-read` and does NOT list `crm.objects.emails.read`.
  - The v3 spec's catalogue (and the 321895/2026-09 spec) does list `crm.objects.emails.read` as an alternative, but community reports say that granular scope cannot be selected in an app's scope settings.
  - HubSpot's changelog introduced `sales-email-read` for email content. Without it, the details of `EMAIL` / `INCOMING_EMAIL` / `FORWARDED_EMAIL` engagement records are redacted (legacy Engagements API), and calls to `/crm/v3/objects/emails` return `403 MISSING_SCOPES` (community report).
  - `sales-email-read` is a read scope, so it is compatible with Product Law 2 (read-only).
  - **Tier:** every Emails spec version carries `x-hubspot-product-tier-requirements` `{marketing: FREE, sales: FREE, service: FREE, cms: FREE, commerce: FREE, crmHub: FREE, dataHub: FREE}`, so no paid tier is needed.
  - Do NOT use the legacy Engagements v1 API (`/engagements/v1/...`); it is absent from HubSpot's current spec collection.
  - **Verifier caveat (the official specs conflict):**
    - The 321895/2026-09 spec, on the email-specific path `GET /crm/objects/2026-09/emails`, accepts `crm.objects.contacts.read` ALONE (or `crm.objects.emails.read` alone) among 79 single-scope alternatives, and does not list `sales-email-read`.
    - The `sales-email-read` requirement appears in exactly one file of the whole collection (424/2026-09). The 424 v3, 2025-09 and 2026-03 Emails specs have empty security.
    - The 424 requirement looks deliberate: in the 424 family, Calls, Notes, Meetings, Tasks, Communications and Postal Mail 2026-09 require only `crm.objects.contacts.read`; only Emails adds `sales-email-read`.
    - Airbyte's production HubSpot connector declares `required_scopes: crm.objects.contacts.read, sales-email-read` for email engagements (community).
    - The recommendation is safe either way: if `crm.objects.contacts.read` alone suffices, `sales-email-read` is a harmless extra read scope; if it is required, omitting it breaks §5.6–5.9.
- **Design consequence:** Make `sales-email-read` a REQUIRED scope (`oauth`, `crm.objects.contacts.read`, `forms`, `sales-email-read`, plus whatever other sections add), not an optional one: HubSpot only drops optional scopes when the portal lacks the product, so the user cannot opt out of it individually. Record in DECISIONS.md that it is read-only but lets the app read one-to-one email content, and say so honestly on the privacy page and in onboarding ("we read only dates and directions, never content"); without it, §5.6–5.9 can only use contact-level date properties (see HS-REPLY-SIGNAL).
- **Open risk:** The developers.hubspot.com scope table, the changelog post and the community 403 report were read only as search summaries (egress blocked). The scrape of the official emails guide has its scope block collapsed, so the docs could not confirm the spec. If HubSpot later makes `crm.objects.emails.read` selectable, it may become the preferred narrower scope. **WIRE_UP step 1:** re-check the app's scope picker, and confirm that an emails read/search call with the configured scopes returns no `403 MISSING_SCOPES` and no redacted records.

### HS-EMAIL-ENDPOINTS-PROPS — Read-only endpoints, search body, limits and email properties
- **Brief:** §5.7 "[VERIFY a read-only signal]"; nothing further.
- **Resolves:** [VERIFY] §5.7 a read-only signal (endpoint and property half; HS-INBOX-LOGGING-CHECK-DESIGN gives the full check).
- **Verdict:** Extended — high confidence.
- **Finding:**
  - **Endpoints** (base `https://api.hubapi.com`; SDK 14.x uses the v3 paths; dated equivalents are `/crm/objects/2026-09/emails...`):
    - `GET /crm/v3/objects/emails?limit=&after=&properties=&associations=&archived=`
    - `GET /crm/v3/objects/emails/{emailId}?properties=&associations=`
    - `POST /crm/v3/objects/emails/search`
    - `POST /crm/v3/objects/emails/batch/read` with body `{inputs:[{id}], properties:[...], propertiesWithHistory:[]}`, max 100 inputs.
  - **Scopes:** `crm.objects.contacts.read` + `sales-email-read` (see HS-EMAIL-SCOPES). **Tier:** FREE.
  - **Search body:** `{filterGroups:[{filters:[{propertyName, operator, value | values | highValue}]}], properties:[...], sorts:[{propertyName, direction: ASCENDING|DESCENDING}] (one sort only), limit<=200, after:"0"}`.
    - The spec types `PublicObjectSearchRequest.sorts` as `string[]` and marks `after`, `filterGroups`, `limit`, `properties` and `sorts` all as required; the docs use the object form `[{propertyName, direction}]`. With raw fetch, send the documented object form and always include every field (`after: "0"` on the first page). The SDK README's TypeScript example uses the string form (`["-createdate"]` for descending).
  - **Operators:** `BETWEEN`, `CONTAINS_TOKEN`, `EQ`, `GT`, `GTE`, `HAS_PROPERTY`, `IN`, `LT`, `LTE`, `NEQ`, `NOT_CONTAINS_TOKEN`, `NOT_HAS_PROPERTY`, `NOT_IN`.
  - **Search limits:**
    - Stay at ≤3 `filterGroups` × ≤3 filters, the strictest official statement. The official sources disagree: Node SDK README "Only 3 `FilterGroups` with max 3 `Filters` are supported"; docs "a maximum of five `filterGroups` with up to six `filters` in each group, with a maximum of 18 filters in total"; spec "Up to 6 groups". Every query Autopilot needs fits in 1 group of ≤3 filters.
    - 10,000 results per query; 5 search requests/s per account; request body ≤3,000 chars; only one sort.
    - Archived records are excluded from search, and there is a short indexing delay ("It may take a few moments for newly created or updated CRM objects to appear in search results").
  - **Response:** `{total, results:[{id, properties{...}, createdAt, updatedAt, archived}], paging?.next.after}` (`CollectionResponseWithTotalSimplePublicObject`, required `[results, total]`). Email search returns by default only `hs_createdate`, `hs_lastmodifieddate`, `hs_object_id`. Requested-but-empty properties come back as `null`.
  - **Email properties:**
    - `hs_timestamp`: activity time; filter with epoch-ms strings.
    - `hs_email_direction`: `EMAIL` ("sent from the CRM or sent and logged to the CRM with the BCC address"), `INCOMING_EMAIL` ("a reply to a logged outgoing email"), `FORWARDED_EMAIL` ("forwarded to the CRM"). The official wording is "logged", not "recorded" as one search summary has it.
    - `hs_email_status`: `BOUNCED` | `FAILED` | `SCHEDULED` | `SENDING` | `SENT`.
    - `hubspot_owner_id`.
    - Read-only, derived from `hs_email_headers`: `hs_email_from_email`, `hs_email_from_firstname`, `hs_email_from_lastname`, `hs_email_to_email`, `hs_email_to_firstname`, `hs_email_to_lastname`.
    - Content (never request): `hs_email_subject`, `hs_email_text`, `hs_email_html`, `hs_email_headers`, `hs_attachment_ids`, `hs_body_preview`.
    - Not filterable: `hs_email_html`, `hs_body_preview`. Never use the free-text `query` field (it searches `hs_email_subject`).
  - **30-day check:** `POST /crm/v3/objects/emails/search` with the body below. Read `total`, then repeat with operator `IN`, values `["INCOMING_EMAIL","FORWARDED_EMAIL"]`.
    ```json
    {"filterGroups":[{"filters":[{"propertyName":"hs_timestamp","operator":"GTE","value":"<nowMs-2592000000>"},{"propertyName":"hs_email_direction","operator":"EQ","value":"EMAIL"}]}],"properties":["hs_timestamp","hs_email_direction"],"sorts":[{"propertyName":"hs_timestamp","direction":"DESCENDING"}],"limit":1,"after":"0"}
    ```
  - **Rate limiting:** a per-portal limiter of 100 requests/10 s for all calls (the SDK README: "Apps using OAuth are only subject to a limit of 100 requests every 10 seconds"), plus ≤5 search requests/s. The SDK's own search limiter is `minTime 550 ms / maxConcurrent 3` (~1.8 req/s).
  - SDK note: the search path is in `codegen/crm/objects/emails/apis/SearchApi.ts`; `BasicApi.getPage(limit?, after?, properties?, propertiesWithHistory?, associations?, archived?)` lives in `BasicApi.ts` in the same directory, not in `SearchApi.ts`.
- **Design consequence:** The `HubSpotClient` interface gets `searchEmails(filters, properties, limit, after) -> {total, results}`, `getContactEmailIds(contactId)` and `batchReadEmails(ids, properties)`, using v3 paths (as SDK 14.x does) with a configurable base path for a later move to `/crm/objects/2026-09`. Rate-limit with a 5 req/s per-portal bucket for search inside a shared 100 requests/10 s per-portal budget for all calls, and use `total` with `limit=1` for the §5.7 counts.
- **Open risk:** The search index is eventually consistent, and this is officially documented: a just-logged email may take a short time to appear in `/search`. The 10-minute live test and follow-up jobs must therefore read via contact associations plus `batch/read` (HS-EMAIL-BY-CONTACT), which reads the record store directly. The three official filter-limit statements conflict, so stay at ≤3×3. The search-guide limits were read only via a search summary and a third-party scrape. **WIRE_UP:** run the 30-day query on a test portal and confirm that the object-form `sorts` is accepted and `total` is returned.

### HS-EMAIL-DATA-MINIMISATION — Every read takes a property allow-list, so content is never fetched
- **Brief:** Law 4: store only IDs, timestamps and statuses; never content.
- **Verdict:** Confirmed — high confidence.
- **Finding:**
  - Every read endpoint takes an explicit property allow-list:
    - GET `properties` query param ("A comma separated list of the properties to be returned in the response.");
    - search body `properties` ("A list of property names to include in the response");
    - `batch/read` body `properties` (required; `BatchReadInputSimplePublicObjectId` requires `["inputs","properties","propertiesWithHistory"]`).
  - Request only `["hs_timestamp","hs_email_direction","hs_email_status"]`, plus `["hs_email_from_email","hs_email_to_email"]` only when you must match the lead's address.
  - Never request `hs_email_subject`, `hs_email_text`, `hs_email_html`, `hs_email_headers`, `hs_attachment_ids`, or `hs_body_preview` (or any `hs_body_preview*` variant; it is a body excerpt).
  - Never use the search `query` field: it full-text-searches `hs_email_subject`.
  - Do not pass the `associations` param on email reads unless needed (harmless but unnecessary).
  - Email search returns by default only `hs_createdate`, `hs_lastmodifieddate` and `hs_object_id`, all non-content.
  - Properties that exist but are empty come back as `null`; treat a `null` `hs_email_status` as "unknown", not as a failure.
  - The OAuth scope (`sales-email-read`) still technically grants content access, so minimisation is enforced by our code, not by HubSpot.
- **Design consequence:** Hard-code an allow-list constant `EMAIL_METADATA_PROPS` in the HubSpot client, with a unit test that fails if any content property name appears in an outgoing request. Compare from/to addresses in memory and never persist them (store only email engagement IDs and timestamps), and have the Sentry scrubber drop HubSpot response bodies.
- **Open risk:** None material. HubSpot returns a few default system properties (`hs_object_id`, `hs_createdate`, `hs_lastmodifieddate`) alongside requested ones; the official search guide confirms they are non-content.

### HS-EMAIL-BY-CONTACT — Reading a contact's emails: contact GET with `associations=emails`, then emails `batch/read`
- **Brief:** not addressed.
- **Verdict:** Extended — high confidence.
- **Finding:**
  - **Pattern A (default for follow-up jobs, 2 calls):**
    1. `GET /crm/v3/objects/contacts/{contactId}?properties=email,hs_additional_emails,hs_email_optout,hs_email_bad_address,hs_email_hard_bounce_reason_enum,hs_sales_email_last_replied&associations=emails`. The response has `associations.emails.results[{id, type}]` (plus `paging.next` if there are more). Scope: `crm.objects.contacts.read`. The same GET detects deletion (404) and merges (the returned `id` differs from the stored one); see HS-CONTACT-DELETED-MERGED.
    2. `POST /crm/v3/objects/emails/batch/read` with `{"inputs":[{"id":"..."}] (<=100 per call),"properties":["hs_timestamp","hs_email_direction","hs_email_status","hs_email_from_email","hs_email_to_email"],"propertiesWithHistory":[]}`. Scopes: `crm.objects.contacts.read` + `sales-email-read`. Filter in memory.
  - **Pattern B (full paging):**
    - `GET /crm/v4/objects/contacts/{contactId}/associations/emails?limit=500&after=...` ("Limit 500 per call"; `limit` default 500) returns `{results:[{toObjectId, associationTypes:[{category, typeId, label}]}], paging?.next.after}`. `category` enum: `HUBSPOT_DEFINED`, `INTEGRATOR_DEFINED`, `USER_DEFINED`, `WORK`. The op accepts `crm.objects.contacts.read` as one of its OR-alternative scopes; tier FREE.
    - Parse `toObjectId` as string-or-number: the spec and SDK type it as string, but production v4 responses are widely observed to return numbers. This is unverified either way, so use `z.union([z.string(), z.number()]).transform(String)`.
    - Type IDs: contact→email `197`, email→contact `198` (official SDK enum and docs table). Reading with `toObjectType=emails` does not need the type ID.
    - For many contacts: `POST /crm/v4/associations/contacts/emails/batch/read` with `{"inputs":[{"id":"<contactId>"}]}` (≤1,000 inputs) returns `results[{from:{id}, to:[{toObjectId, associationTypes}], paging?}]`.
  - **Pattern C (single search call, subject to indexing lag):** `POST /crm/v3/objects/emails/search` with filter `{"propertyName":"associations.contact","operator":"EQ","value":"<contactId>"}` plus `hs_timestamp` / `hs_email_direction` filters. The `associations.{objectType}` pseudo-property is documented generically; HubSpot gives custom-object associations as the only exclusion.
  - **Rate limits:** the association API burst limit on Free and Starter is 100 requests/10 s; object batch endpoints take ≤100 inputs ("Object API batch endpoints are limited to 100 inputs per request", an official guide statement, not just a convention); v4 association batch read takes ≤1,000 inputs.
- **Design consequence:** Implement `getContactEmailActivity(contactId, sinceMs)` as Pattern A (switching to Pattern B paging when the contact has more associated emails than one response returns), then `batch/read` in chunks of 100, then filter in code by `hs_timestamp > sinceMs` and direction; this path is spec-backed and avoids search-index lag, which matters for the 10-minute live test and follow-up jobs. ### HS-REPLY-SIGNAL — A reply is an `INCOMING_EMAIL`/`FORWARDED_EMAIL` engagement from the lead, not a contact timestamp
- **Brief:** §5.6: "Use the reply signal identified in RESEARCH (e.g. a last-replied timestamp newer than the first notification)".
- **Resolves:** §5.6 "the reply signal identified in RESEARCH" (a RESEARCH dependency rather than a formal [VERIFY] marker).
- **Verdict:** Corrected — medium confidence. The engagement-based rule rests on official developer docs (via a verbatim scrape); the owner-setup requirements are not officially documented here (KB search summaries only).
- **Finding:**
  - **Rule.** Reply confirmed for lead L (contact C, primary email E, `hs_additional_emails` A) after T = `first_notified_at` when:
    - **PRIMARY:** any email engagement associated to C (HS-EMAIL-BY-CONTACT) has `hs_email_direction` in {`INCOMING_EMAIL`, `FORWARDED_EMAIL`}, `hs_timestamp` > T, and `lower(hs_email_from_email)` in `{lower(E)} ∪ lower(A)`. Scopes: `crm.objects.contacts.read` + `sales-email-read`.
    - **FALLBACK** (positive-only, contacts scope): `contact.hs_sales_email_last_replied` > T. A null or older value never proves "no reply".
    - Otherwise: "no confirmed reply". Never "no reply".
  - **Official basis:** the emails guide defines `INCOMING_EMAIL` as "the email was a reply to a logged outgoing email" and `FORWARDED_EMAIL` as "the email was forwarded to the CRM". So the reply signal must be an engagement, and a missing engagement never proves "no reply".
  - **Why the from-address match:** a logged email can be associated to several contacts (e.g. the lead CC'd on someone else's thread). Accepting an `INCOMING_EMAIL` only when `hs_email_from_email` (case-insensitive) equals the lead's email or one of `hs_additional_emails` avoids false "replied" stops from third-party emails, which would wrongly stop follow-ups.
  - **Direction of "Log all" inbound mail:** the direction value HubSpot assigns to inbound mail logged by the "Log all" rule (as opposed to replies on logged threads) is not documented. Accept `INCOMING_EMAIL` and `FORWARDED_EMAIL` from the lead; ignore `EMAIL`.
  - **Do NOT use as reply signals:** `hs_email_last_reply_date` (marketing-email statistic), `notes_last_contacted`, `hs_last_sales_activity_timestamp`, `num_contacted_notes` (see HS-CONTACT-ACTIVITY-PROPS).
  - **`hs_sales_email_last_replied` definitions conflict:** the KB summary says "the date of the last time that the contact sent an email to your connected personal or team email"; an older property export says "The last time a tracked sales email was replied to by this user." Either way it is positive-only, so the fallback is fine.
  - **Owner-setup requirements** (from KB search summaries, UNVERIFIED; confirm in WIRE_UP on a Free test portal):
    - (a) A connected personal inbox plus the account rule "Log all emails to/from known contacts" logs both sends from the native/mobile mail app and the lead's replies.
    - (b) A connected inbox with "Log replies only" logs replies only on threads that started from the CRM or the Sales extension/add-in, so compose-link sends are not covered.
    - (c) BCC only logs the send, not the reply, unless the owner forwards the reply to the forwarding address (logged as `FORWARDED_EMAIL`).
  - Onboarding must measure this (the §5.7 live test with a reply leg), not assume it.
- **Design consequence:** At each follow-up job run the PRIMARY check, then the FALLBACK, else record "no confirmed reply" (never infer "no reply" from missing data, Law 3); store a per-portal `logging_mode = connected_log_all | connected_replies_only | bcc_only | none` detected from the §5.7 test and show it in settings and the dashboard, showing "Replies detected automatically" only for a connected inbox with "Log all emails to/from known contacts". For BCC-only owners show a clear warning ("HubSpot will log your sends but NOT the lead's replies; follow-ups may go to people who already replied unless you forward replies to <forwarding address>"), consider defaulting follow-ups OFF in that mode (record the choice in DECISIONS.md), and say in follow-up copy that replies may be missed in BCC mode.
- **Open risk:**
  - (a) The default value of the contact email logging rule is unclear: the launch announcement said the default is "Log replies to logged threads only", while a later KB snippet implies "Log all". Onboarding must not assume either.
  - (b) The KB "Applies to" line for the logging-rules article was not captured; a community source says all hubs/tiers.
  - (c) Out-of-office auto-replies are logged as incoming emails and would stop follow-ups; we can't detect them without reading content. Offer a "Resume follow-ups" override.
  - (d) Whether a reply to a BCC-logged send is ever auto-logged with a connected inbox under "Log replies only" is not documented; treat it as no.
  - (e) `hs_sales_email_last_replied` ("tracked sales email") semantics suggest it isn't updated for untracked native-app sends.
  - All owner-setup claims (connected inbox on all plans, the "Log all" rule and its wording, the "Log replies only" conditions, Never Log, the permissions) rest only on knowledge.hubspot.com search summaries. **WIRE_UP:** check them on a Free test portal; the §5.7 live test is the real verification.
